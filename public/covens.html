COVENS

Band together, hold the longest night, claim the Manor.

A coven is your circle in Thornreach — up to eight witches, werewolves and liches who share a sigil, a home, and a run at the crown. Band together and the whole town plays differently.

🌑

A private world

Every coven gets its own private copy of the Wilds — your circle's own world, unseen by the public town and shared by the whole circle.

🏰

Your own Manor

An eight-bedroom mansion in your private Wilds, with a séance altar and shared storage. The reigning champion claims it.

💰

A shared Bank

Pool gold and up to twelve item slots for the whole circle to draw on.

🕯️

Weekly Rites

The seasonal Wheel-of-the-Year questline, run together — a new Rite turns with every sabbat.

👑

Standings & Crowns

Climb the weekly coven leaderboard. The champion coven takes a 750-gold purse and the crown.

🎙️

Voice & chat

A members-only chat channel and coven voice — in the game and right here on the web.

🤝 Join a circle

Free — just need an invite

Get an invite from any coven leader and you're in: the private world, the Manor, the Bank, the Rites, voice, and the champion's run are all yours to share. Browse the living circles below, or find one in town.
