PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated 12 October 2026 · Effective 31 October 2026
Thornreach is a small, independent game. This policy is written in plain language to tell you honestly what the game does with your data. It is a clear starting point, not formal legal advice, and we expect to refine the wording with a lawyer as the game grows. If anything here is unclear, just ask us.

1 · The short version

  • You can create an account with just a username and password. An email is optional and only used to recover your password.
  • We don't run ad trackers or analytics, and we don't sell your data. There are no advertising cookies.
  • Some features — the werewolf's "howl" and the witch's "selfie" — record your microphone or camera, but only after you tap a consent prompt each time, and only for that one feature.
  • Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time — see Your choices.

2 · Who we are

Thornreach ("the game", "we", "us") is operated by the Thornreach team. This policy covers the Thornreach website and the game at thornreach, however you reach them. For anything about your data, contact us at privacy@thornreach.com.

3 · What we collect

We try to collect as little as possible. Here's the full list.

Account information

Username
Chosen by you and shown publicly in-game and on the site.
Password
Stored only as a salted hash — we never keep the password itself.
Email (optional)
Only if you add one, and only used to send a password-reset link. Reset links are stored hashed.
Age confirmation
That you confirmed you are 18 or older, with the date and the IP address you confirmed from.

Gameplay data

Your characters, levels, story progress, in-game gold, inventory, coven membership, achievements, and leaderboard standings. This is what makes your account yours, and some of it (name, class, level, coven, honors) is shown publicly on your profile and the leaderboards.

Technical & security data

To keep the town safe from abuse, we keep a security log of events like failed logins and rate-limit trips. These records can include your IP address and the time. We use them only to protect accounts and the service — not to build a profile of you.

Payments

If you buy a Town Pass or a coven charter, checkout is handled by Stripe. Your card details go straight to Stripe — we never receive or store them. We keep only a payment reference so we can confirm your purchase. In-game gold is not real money, has no cash value, and can't be cashed out.

Notifications (optional)

If you turn on browser notifications (for events like moonrise), your browser gives us a push "subscription" so we can send them. You can turn this off in your browser at any time.

On your device

We store a small login token and your display name in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. This is not an advertising or tracking cookie — it's only used to keep you logged in and lives on your device.

4 · Selfies, howls & voice — read this one

A few optional features capture real media. We want to be especially clear about these.

The witch's "selfie"

In one in-game trade, you may choose to take a selfie with your camera. If you do:

  • It happens only after you agree to the trade — nothing is captured unless you opt in.
  • The photo is sent to Anthropic's Claude vision service for a one-time automated check that it actually shows a human face (to keep the feature from being abused). Anthropic processes it to answer that check.
  • If you then list it, the selfie is shown to other players on the in-game Auction House. Treat anything you list as public.
  • Only upload a selfie of yourself. Don't capture or upload anyone else's face.

The werewolf's "howl"

In another optional trade, you may record a few seconds from your microphone. It happens only after a consent prompt, captures just that short clip, and — if you list it — can be heard by other players on the Auction House. Again: record only your own voice.

Live coven voice chat

Coven voice chat is peer-to-peer (WebRTC). Your audio streams directly between you and your covenmates and is not recorded or stored on our servers — we only pass along the brief messages that let two browsers connect.

5 · How we use it

  • To run your account, save your progress, and show your public profile, covens, and leaderboards.
  • To process purchases and confirm your Town Pass.
  • To keep the game working and protect it from cheating, abuse, and attacks.
  • To send you notifications you asked for, and password resets you request.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it.

6 · Who we share it with

We don't sell or trade your personal data. We rely on a few service providers to run the game, and data passes through them only for that purpose:

Stripe
Payment processing. Receives your payment details directly at checkout.
Anthropic (Claude)
One-time automated "is this a real face?" check on a selfie you choose to take.
Cloudflare
Sits in front of the site to serve pages and block attacks; sees traffic and IP addresses.
Render
Hosts the game and its assets.
Google Fonts & cdnjs
Serve fonts and a code library; your browser's IP is visible to them when a page loads.
Google Play
If you buy through an Android app, Google handles that purchase.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety and rights of players and the game.

7 · How long we keep it

We keep your account and gameplay data for as long as your account exists. Security logs are kept for a limited period and then rotated out. Media you list on the Auction House stays until the listing ends or you remove it. When you delete your account (see below), we remove your account and the personal data tied to it, except anything we're required to keep for legal or accounting reasons (such as payment records).

8 · Your choices & rights

  • Delete your account & data: open your profile, scroll to "Delete account," and confirm — it erases your account and the data tied to it right away, no waiting on us. (You can also email privacy@thornreach.com if you'd rather we do it.)
  • See or correct your data: ask us and we'll help.
  • Notifications: turn browser notifications off any time in your browser settings.
  • Media: don't use the selfie or howl features if you'd rather not — they're entirely optional, and you can remove a listing you made.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights over your data (for example under the GDPR or CCPA). We'll honor valid requests — just reach out.

9 · How we protect it

Passwords are salted and hashed, not stored in the clear. Password-reset links are stored hashed. Backups are encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data and we limit what we collect in the first place.

10 · Age requirement

Thornreach is for players 18 and older. You confirm your age when you register. The game is not directed to children, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

11 · Changes to this policy

As the game grows we'll update this policy and change the "last updated" date above. If we make a significant change, we'll make a reasonable effort to let players know.

12 · Contact

Questions, requests, or concerns about your data: privacy@thornreach.com. See also our Terms of Service.
