The craft

Keep the old ways

Thornreach is built on real witchcraft — the practices, not the costume. The moon keeps its calendar, the sabbats turn the year, and at Samhain the veil thins and the dead walk closer to the torchlight.

🕯️

The Ancestor Altar

Set a place at the table for your dead, lay an offering in silence — the dumb supper — and carry a forebear's blessing with you.

🔥

The Release Fire

At the turning of the year, name what you carry — a grief, a fear, an old anger — and give it to the flames. Walk away lighter.

🎭

Guising

Pull on a disguise — a bone mask, a jack-o'-head, a horned little devil, a crown of antlers — and the whole town sees you wear it.

🪞

The Scrying Bowl

Gaze into dark water and read the turning wheel — the moon, the Blood Moon's next rise, the sabbat to come — and the day's omen.

🌒

The Moon's Phases

The waxing and waning moon colours every rite, and moon water charges only beneath a full moon.

🌿

A living craft

Keep a herb garden, brew teas by their planetary day, bond a familiar, and walk one of the old paths — green, lunar, hedge, kitchen, sea or hearth.

🔮

Daily Tarot

Lay a three-card spread from the Major Arcana each day — past, present, and what comes — for a reading, a little gold, and the occasional whispered secret.

✨

Altar & correspondence

Dress the altar to the day, the moon and the sabbat, make your offering, and the rite you work rings truer for it.
